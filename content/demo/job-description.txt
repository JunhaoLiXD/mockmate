Computer Vision Engineer (Entry Level), Computational Imaging Lab
Quillmoor Biosciences (fictional) | Boston, MA (Hybrid) | Full-time

ABOUT QUILLMOOR
Quillmoor Biosciences builds automated microscopy systems that photograph
living cells thousands of times a day. Our software measures how cells grow,
divide, move, and respond to drugs, and our pharmaceutical partners use these
measurements to decide which drug candidates move forward. That means our
image analysis has to be accurate, reproducible, and fast.

ABOUT THE TEAM
The Computational Imaging Lab is an 8-person team of biologists, machine
learning researchers, and engineers, led by Dr. Albert Hale. We turn raw,
noisy microscope images into clean, trustworthy data that scientists can use
every morning.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Build and improve image-enhancement tools for microscopy images, including
  contrast correction, low-light enhancement, and denoising.
- Combine classical image processing (for example histogram equalization and
  edge-preserving filters) with deep learning models where they help.
- Turn research prototypes into reliable, modular pipelines that run every
  night on new data.
- Build and maintain internal web tools and APIs that let scientists upload
  images, choose processing steps, and download results.
- Design evaluations that show a method works on real lab data, not just on a
  benchmark, and explain the results clearly to biologists.
- Choose methods that work on real lab data under time limits, and document
  the trade-offs.

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related
  field (new graduates welcome).
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Experience with image processing using OpenCV and NumPy.
- Experience training or using deep learning models with PyTorch.
- Understanding of core data structures, algorithms, and software design.
- Experience with Git.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience building REST APIs (for example with FastAPI or Flask).
- Experience building a front end with React or TypeScript.
- Experience deploying an application to a cloud service.
- Experience with low-light enhancement, denoising, or image restoration.
- Ability to explain technical results to people without a technical
  background.
- Interest in biology or scientific imaging (no biology background required).

WHAT WE VALUE
- Real understanding of what you built, beyond knowing how to call a library.
- Honest evaluation: knowing how and why a method fails.
- Clear thinking about trade-offs between quality, speed, and cost.
- Clear communication with teammates from different backgrounds.
